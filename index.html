A structured desktop workspace for AI Remote Viewing research.

AI RV Harness combines blind RV sessions, ordinary Conversations, structured Training, controlled Research, separate AI roles, target management, evidence-preserving Reveal flows, and reproducible evaluation tools.

WHAT IT IS

One environment, several deliberately separated roles.

Conversations and blind sessions

Keep ordinary Conversations separate from controlled RV Sessions while preserving the boundary between Viewer evidence and Reveal information.

Structured Training

Use bundled and user-provided targets, resumable checkpoints, Viewer self-review, Viewer Notes, and optional judging.

Controlled Research

Build locked, blinded studies with reproducible designs, frozen evidence, judging, unblinding, and experiment statistics.

AI Center

Keep AI Monitor history, AI identities, Viewer Notes, and versioned Viewer Learning resources in distinct profile-wide tools.

Local-first records

The desktop application stores project state locally with SQLite while remote providers are used only when selected.

Evidence boundaries

Viewer, Monitor, Judge, Research, and post-Reveal material are handled through explicit workflow boundaries rather than one blended chat.

PROJECT BOUNDARY

A research workflow, not a claim of proof.

AI RV Harness does not claim that Remote Viewing has been scientifically proven, that a particular model possesses anomalous perception, or that model-generated material is accurate.

The project provides controlled procedures, records, and evaluation tools. Interpretation remains the responsibility of the user and researcher.
