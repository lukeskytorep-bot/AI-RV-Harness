# Target 0089

**Target ID:** 391K GR44  
**Date of the target:** Present day (Constructed 447–432 BC)  

**Target:** The Parthenon, located on the Acropolis of Athens, Greece (37° 58′ 17″ N, 23° 43′ 36″ E).

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a monumental ancient classical ruin. The scene features the skeletal remains of a massive marble temple characterized by standing columns and architraves, positioned on the flattened summit of a steep, rocky citadel overlooking a sprawling modern metropolis and a distant coastal bay.

---

### Part 1: The Monumental Architecture

*   **The Structure:** The central building is the Parthenon, a massive rectangular temple constructed primarily of Pentelic marble. It is heavily weathered, showing centuries of structural damage, partial dismantling, and the effects of a massive 17th-century explosive blast.
*   **The Columns:** The perimeter is defined by a colonnade of massive, fluted Doric columns. While the outer colonnades are largely intact or restored, the inner sanctuary (cella) and the entire roof structure are completely missing.
*   **Upper Elements:** Surviving structural elements above the columns include sections of the horizontal architrave, frieze blocks, and the triangular pediments at the eastern and western ends. Modern metal scaffolding is often visible, indicating ongoing preservation efforts.

### Part 2: The Acropolis Citadel

*   **The Plateau:** The temple sits atop a high, naturally fortified limestone outcrop known as the Acropolis. The summit is relatively flat, barren, and completely covered with loose, fragmented marble blocks, carved architectural debris, and exposed bedrock.
*   **The Cliffs:** The edges of the Acropolis feature steep, sheer vertical drops. These cliff faces are heavily fortified by massive, ancient, stone retaining walls that encircle the plateau.
*   **Lower Slopes:** The immediate base of the steep citadel is heavily forested with patches of pine, cypress, and olive trees, providing a green, natural buffer around the ancient archaeological site.

### Part 3: The Urban and Coastal Environment

*   **The City:** Beyond the immediate slopes of the Acropolis, the modern city of Athens stretches out in all directions. It appears as an incredibly dense, tightly packed urban sprawl composed of thousands of light-colored, flat-roofed buildings extending to the horizon.
*   **The Distant View:** Because of the extreme elevation of the Acropolis hill, the vantage point from the very top offers a commanding panoramic view. Looking past the sprawling urban landscape, the waters of a distinct coastal bay (the Saronic Gulf) can be clearly seen far in the distance.
