# Target 0084

**Target ID:** 492A SP20  
**Date of the target:** October 2020  
**Target coordinates:** 42.6193° N, 0.5448° E (Posets-Maladeta Natural Park, near Benasque, Huesca, Spain).

**Target:** A high-altitude, 360-degree aerial panorama (drone perspective) of a sweeping glacial valley and rugged peaks in the Pyrenees mountains.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a vast, high-altitude natural landscape captured from a suspended, unanchored aerial vantage point. The scene highlights the dramatic topographic scale of a major mountain range, contrasting the imposing, barren rock of the upper peaks with the dense, dark forests and human infrastructure nestled deep in the valley below.

---

### Part 1: The Aerial Perspective and Terrain

*   **The Vantage Point:** The perspective is completely detached from the ground, suspended hundreds of feet in the air above a steep mountain slope, providing an unobstructed macro view of the surrounding geology.
*   **The Mountains:** The landscape is dominated by massive, steeply pitched mountain peaks. The upper elevations are characterized by exposed, jagged, light-grey and tan bedrock, featuring sharp ridges and deep erosion gullies. 
*   **The Horizon:** The extreme distant background features further layers of mountain ranges, some of which exhibit patches of white snow or glaciers near their summits.

### Part 2: Vegetation and Valley Floor

*   **The Forests:** The lower and mid-level slopes of the mountains are heavily blanketed by thick, continuous coniferous forests, appearing dark green in the landscape. 
*   **The Nadir View:** Looking straight down from the aerial position, the terrain consists of a textured canopy of pine trees interspersed with geometric, light-green clearings and grassy meadows.
*   **The Valley:** Between the towering peaks lies a deep, sweeping U-shaped valley. The floor of the valley is relatively flat and green, containing agricultural patches and natural water runoff.

### Part 3: Hydrology and Human Infrastructure

*   **The Settlement:** Located far in the distance on the flat valley floor is a small, tightly clustered mountain town (Benasque). Its buildings appear tiny from the extreme aerial perspective.
*   **The Infrastructure:** A winding, paved two-lane road traces the bottom of the valley, running parallel to the natural course of the river. 
*   **The Reservoir:** To the right of the main valley axis, nestled between forested slopes, is a small, distinctively blue artificial reservoir (Embalse de Linsoles) held back by a dam structure.
