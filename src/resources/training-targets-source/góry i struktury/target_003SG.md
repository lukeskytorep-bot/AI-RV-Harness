# Target 0086

**Target ID:** 912C SP19  
**Target coordinates:** 42.8986° N, 0.7635° W (Barranco de Petrechema, Ansó, Huesca Province, Spain)  
**Date of the target:** September 2019  

**Target:** A high-altitude mountain ravine and hiking trail in the Valles Occidentales Natural Park, Spanish Pyrenees.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a rugged alpine valley environment characterized by stark geological transitions. The scene captures a deep ravine enclosed by sheer limestone cliffs, massive accumulations of fragmented rock, and scattered coniferous vegetation under a bright, clear sky.

---

### Part 1: Topography and Geological Features

*   **The Ravine:** The central environment is a steep, U-shaped mountain valley (a *barranco*). The terrain is highly uneven, transitioning from a relatively flat, grassy base to steep, dramatic inclines.
*   **The Cliffs:** The valley is flanked by massive, stratified limestone cliff faces. These walls feature distinct horizontal banding and heavy vertical weathering, exposing pale grey and tan bedrock.
*   **The Scree Slopes:** At the base of the sheer cliffs are extensive scree (talus) slopes. These are massive, unstable accumulations of loose, fragmented rock and boulders that have eroded and tumbled down from the upper ridges, forming steep, fan-shaped rock fields.

### Part 2: Vegetation and Flora

*   **The Valley Floor:** The lowest visible elevations and the immediate foreground are covered in dry, yellow-green alpine grasses and low-lying scrub brush.
*   **Coniferous Trees:** Clinging selectively to the rocky slopes and the edges of the scree fields are scattered, dark-green coniferous trees (predominantly mountain pines). They grow in isolated clusters, demonstrating the harsh, nutrient-poor conditions of the rocky terrain.
*   **Lower Valley Forest:** Looking back down the ravine (toward the lower elevations), the vegetation thickens significantly into a denser, continuous green forest canopy.

### Part 3: Human Presence and Atmosphere

*   **The Trail:** A narrow, unpaved dirt and rock hiking path winds its way through the center of the grassy valley floor, navigating around the edges of the rock falls.
*   **Infrastructure:** The environment is deeply remote with minimal infrastructure. A single, simple wooden trail marker post with a bright green directional sign is placed alongside the path to guide hikers.
*   **Atmospheric Conditions:** The sky is a vast, clear, vibrant blue, dotted with only a few faint, wispy contrails. The scene is illuminated by intense, direct sunlight, casting deep, sharp black shadows behind the massive rock formations and trees on the right side of the valley.
