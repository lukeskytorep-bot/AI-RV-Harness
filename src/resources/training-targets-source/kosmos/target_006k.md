# Target 0096

**Target ID:** 447L CM72  
**Date of the target:** December 14, 1972  

**Target:** The Apollo 17 Lunar Module "Challenger" (ascent stage) in lunar orbit.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a manned orbital spacecraft module operating in the vacuum of space. The scene captures a highly angular, asymmetrical vehicle composed of flat metallic panels and crinkled thermal foil, isolated entirely against the absolute blackness of deep space.

---

### Part 1: The Ascent Stage Geometry

*   **The Structure:** The central object is the ascent stage of a lunar lander. Dictated strictly by the vacuum environment, its structure lacks any aerodynamic shaping. It is highly irregular and faceted, consisting of intersecting flat geometric planes and sharp, unstreamlined angles.
*   **Surface Paneling:** The exterior hull is a patchwork of different materials, predominantly light grey and silver metallic panels, juxtaposed with contrasting dark, non-reflective sections. 
*   **Features:** Small, dark, recessed areas and specific geometric cutouts (including triangular and circular window ports) are set into the forward-facing facets of the module.

### Part 2: External Hardware and Interface

*   **Communications Array:** Protruding prominently from the top-left of the module (relative to the camera) is a flat, circular dish antenna mounted on a short structural mast. The dish features a distinct dark, cross-shaped element at its center.
*   **Propulsion Systems:** Extending outward from the left and right sides of the angular hull are distinct mechanical outriggers. Each outrigger terminates in a cluster of small, cone-shaped maneuvering thrusters (Reaction Control System quads) pointing in multiple directions.
*   **The Separation Plane:** The underside of the module is highly irregular and jagged. It is characterized by heavily crumpled, highly reflective gold, copper, and orange-colored thermal foil (Kapton). This rough lower surface indicates the mechanical separation plane where the vehicle detached from its descent stage.

### Part 3: The Orbital Environment

*   **The Vacuum:** The spacecraft is suspended in a state of free-fall microgravity.
*   **The Backdrop:** The entire background surrounding the vehicle is a solid, unbroken expanse of absolute, lightless black. 
*   **Lighting:** There are no atmospheric features, planetary horizons, or visible stars in the frame. This emphasizes the stark, high-contrast lighting of the sunlit spacecraft, creating sharp, hard-edged shadows across its complex geometric facets.
