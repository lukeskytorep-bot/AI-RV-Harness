# Target 0091

**Target ID:** 614R MS96  
**Date of the target:** September 24, 1996  

**Target:** The Russian Mir space station in low Earth orbit, as viewed from the Space Shuttle Atlantis.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of an artificial, modular orbital habitat operating in the vacuum of space. The scene captures a highly complex piece of space infrastructure, characterized by interconnected pressurized cylinders and extensive energy-gathering arrays, suspended against the vast backdrop of Earth's atmosphere.

---

### Part 1: The Core Architecture and Modules

*   **The Structure:** The space station is an asymmetrical, multi-modular construction. It is composed of several distinct cylindrical pressure modules joined together at central docking nodes, creating a cross-like or radiating geometric layout.
*   **Surface Materials:** The exterior of the core modules is highly textured, covered predominantly in white thermal insulation blankets, rigid metallic shielding, and exterior handrails.
*   **Docked Spacecraft:** At the very top (relative to the camera's orientation) of the central vertical axis, a smaller, distinctively bright orange spacecraft (a Soyuz or Progress vehicle) is securely docked to the station.

### Part 2: Solar Arrays and External Hardware

*   **Solar Panels:** The most prominent visual features are the numerous large, flat, dark-blue rectangular solar arrays protruding from the various modules. 
*   **Array Geometry:** These grid-like panels extend in multiple directions to maximize solar exposure. One massive array extends straight upward, parallel to the central module. The right-side module features arrays deployed in a distinct V-shape, while the left-side module has panels extending perpendicularly.
*   **External Boom:** Extending outward and downward from the left-hand module is a long, thin, rigid lattice-like mechanical boom or crane structure.

### Part 3: The Orbital Environment (Background)

*   **The Vantage Point:** The perspective is captured from a separate spacecraft (Space Shuttle Atlantis) flying in close proximity to the station, looking slightly downward at the target.
*   **The Atmosphere:** The entire background of the image is filled by the Earth from a high-altitude orbital perspective.
*   **Cloud Cover:** The planet's surface is completely obscured by a vast, continuous, and highly textured layer of white and pale blue clouds. The intense, unfiltered sunlight casts distinct shadows across the cloud tops, emphasizing their depth and meteorological structure.
