# Target 0094

**Target ID:** 832E AE72  
**Date of the target:** December 17, 1972  

**Target:** Astronaut Ronald Evans performing a deep-space extravehicular activity (EVA) outside the Apollo 17 Command and Service Module during the trans-Earth coast.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a human subject operating in the absolute vacuum of deep space. The scene captures a tethered astronaut floating in microgravity directly adjacent to the highly textured, metallic exterior hull of an orbital spacecraft. The environment is entirely devoid of atmospheric light, defined by the stark contrast between the illuminated subject and the pitch-black void.

---

### Part 1: The Extravehicular Mobility Unit (Spacesuit)

*   **The Suit:** The human figure is encased in a pressurized, multi-layered white spacesuit designed to sustain life in a vacuum. A prominent United States flag patch is visible on the left shoulder. The lower arms and hands are enclosed in thick, dark grey/black pressurized gloves.
*   **The Helmet and Backpack:** The astronaut's head is protected by a rigid helmet featuring a thick red vertical stripe over the crown. The face is completely concealed by a highly reflective, gold-mirrored visor that strongly reflects the surrounding light and spacecraft structures. A large, box-like life-support and oxygen purge backpack is mounted to the rear of the suit.
*   **Kinetic State:** The astronaut is suspended in a state of free-fall, oriented face-down horizontally relative to the surface of the spacecraft. 

### Part 2: The Spacecraft Interface and Tether

*   **The Umbilical:** A critical life-line is visible in the form of a thick, white, coiled umbilical tether. It snakes outward from the astronaut's right side, floating in a loose loop before connecting to the spacecraft's life-support systems.
*   **The Interaction:** The astronaut's heavily gloved hands are extended downward, hovering directly over and interacting with the complex mechanical surface of the spacecraft.

### Part 3: The Spacecraft Hull and Environment

*   **The Hull:** The lower third of the scene is dominated by the curved, metallic exterior of the Apollo Service Module (specifically the Scientific Instrument Module or SIM bay). The surface is a chaotic grid of reflective metal panels, exposed wiring, recessed equipment bays, and rigid external handholds used for navigation. 
*   **Foreground Hardware:** In the immediate lower-right foreground, out of focus, is a curved, metallic ring or antenna structure protruding from the hull.
*   **The Void:** The entire upper and background area of the image is completely filled by the absolute, unbroken blackness of cislunar space, with no planetary bodies or atmospheric scattering visible.
