# Target 0095

**Target ID:** 372W SA84  
**Date of the target:** November 14, 1984  

**Target:** Astronauts Dale A. Gardner and Joseph P. Allen IV securing the recaptured Westar VI satellite in the payload bay of the Space Shuttle Discovery.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of two human subjects performing a complex extravehicular activity (EVA) in microgravity. The scene captures a highly coordinated mechanical interaction between tethered astronauts, a large cylindrical satellite, and a robotic articulating arm, all suspended against the stark contrast of the Earth's horizon and the absolute vacuum of space.

---

### Part 1: The Human Subjects and Support Hardware

*   **The Subjects:** Two human figures are encased in bulky, pressurized white Extravehicular Mobility Units (spacesuits). Their faces are completely concealed by highly reflective, gold-mirrored visors that catch intense, unfiltered solar flares.
*   **Left Figure (Gardner):** Suspended in microgravity to the left of the central axis, this figure is physically holding a rectangular placard bearing the text "FOR SALE". A thin safety tether extends downward from the lower torso.
*   **Right Figure (Allen):** This figure is anchored by the feet to a specialized foot restraint platform. 
*   **The Robotic Arm:** The foot platform holding the right figure is attached to the end effector of a long, articulated, white cylindrical structure (the Remote Manipulator System, or RMS arm), which extends diagonally into the frame from the upper right.

### Part 2: The Satellite and Payload Bay

*   **The Satellite:** Dominating the lower-center of the field of view is a massive, geometric cylindrical object (the Westar VI satellite). Its exterior features dark, grid-like curvature interrupted by a distinct horizontal red band.
*   **Upper Satellite Structure:** The top surface of the cylinder is heavily wrapped in crinkled, highly reflective gold and silver thermal foil. A prominent, smooth silver spike or antenna extends vertically from the center of this foiled upper section.
*   **The Interface:** The base of the satellite rests within the rigid, complex metallic confines of the space shuttle's open payload bay, which is visible in the lower-left corner as a dense grid of structural components and latches.

### Part 3: The Orbital Environment

*   **The Vacuum:** The upper-left diagonal half of the background is completely dominated by the absolute, lightless black vacuum of space.
*   **The Planetary Horizon:** The lower-right diagonal half of the background features the distinct, sweeping curvature of the Earth. A bright, vivid blue atmospheric band sharply transitions into the void, while the planet's surface is covered by a dense, textured layer of swirling white clouds.
