# Target 0101

**Target ID:** 902X RB12  
**Date of the target:** October 14, 2012  

**Target:** The Red Bull Stratos mission, featuring Felix Baumgartner's record-breaking stratospheric freefall jump from the edge of space.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of an extreme high-altitude aerospace event. The scene captures a human subject in a pressurized life-support suit stepping from a suspended capsule into the absolute vacuum of the stratosphere, aiming to break the sound barrier in a freefall descent toward the Earth's curved horizon.

---

### Part 1: The High-Altitude Ascent Vehicle

*   **The Balloon:** The ascent platform is suspended by a massive stratospheric helium balloon. The balloon's envelope was exceptionally fragile—10 times thinner than a plastic bag—weighing 1,682 kg and holding 835,000 cubic meters of helium. Its total surface area was equivalent to 33 football fields.
*   **The Capsule:** Attached below the balloon is a custom-built, pressurized capsule that transported the human subject to an altitude of 39,969.4 meters (approximately 127,500 feet). 
*   **The Environment:** This deployment altitude is positioned in the stratosphere, vastly exceeding the 63,000-foot Armstrong line—the altitude where atmospheric pressure is so low that human blood would boil without a pressurized environment.

### Part 2: The Human Interface (Pressure Suit)

*   **The Suit:** The human subject (Felix Baumgartner) is equipped with a highly specialized, low-mass pressure suit manufactured by the David Clark Company. 
*   **Thermal and Life Support:** The suit was engineered to withstand the extreme, lethal stratospheric temperatures plummeting to -72°C. It was equipped with an independent oxygen supply designed to last for 20 minutes, covering the critical moments in the depressurized capsule, the jump, and the flight back to Earth.
*   **Design Purpose:** The surprisingly slim profile of the suit allowed for increased mobility, acting as a prototype and learning model for future military and aerospace pressure suits.

### Part 3: The Kinetic Event (Freefall and Landing)

*   **The Jump:** The kinetic focal point is the moment the subject steps off the capsule's exterior platform into the void. The primary objective was to become the first human to break the sound barrier (Mach 1) in freefall without the aid of an aircraft.
*   **Supersonic Velocity:** During the descent, the subject accelerated rapidly in the thin atmosphere, reaching supersonic speeds in just 34 seconds and achieving a maximum velocity of 1,358 km/h.
*   **The Descent:** The entire return trip to the surface took 9 minutes. The subject spent half of this time in pure freefall before successfully deploying a parachute to safely navigate the denser lower atmosphere and land on the ground.
