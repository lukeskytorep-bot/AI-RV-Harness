# Target 0099

**Target ID:** 717N FL07  
**Date of the target:** 18.08.2026 (Opened in 2007)  
**Target coordinates:** 3.25 miles (5.2 km) off the coast of Key Biscayne, Florida, USA  

**Target:** The Neptune Memorial Reef, an underwater columbarium and artificial reef.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a submerged artificial reef functioning as an aquatic cremation memorial site. Located roughly 40 feet (12 metres) beneath the ocean's surface, the environment consists of classical architectural ruins—including gates, fluted columns, and statues—fully immersed in an active marine ecosystem.

---

### Part 1: The Sunken Architecture

*   **The Layout:** The site is structurally designed to resemble a lost city resting on the ocean floor, complete with underwater roads, intersecting pathways, and centralized focal points featuring benches.
*   **Structural Elements:** The architecture utilizes classical and ornate designs, prominently featuring massive, textured columns supporting arched overhead structures, as well as large, decorative metal-styled gates.
*   **Statuary:** Imposing sculptural forms are present, most notably large, highly detailed statues of lions resting their paws on spherical objects, securely mounted on solid rectangular bases.

### Part 2: The Memorial Integration

*   **The Columbarium:** The structures serve a dual purpose as an aquatic columbarium for human remains, moving beyond the definition of a simple underwater cemetery or mausoleum.
*   **Construction Material:** Cremated ashes are mixed directly with specially designed underwater cement, fitted into molds, and then placed and secured by divers to physically form the structural features of the reef.
*   **Plaques and Markers:** The site is heavily decorated with copper and bronze memorial plaques. These include individual markers denoting names, birth/death dates, and personal messages, alongside large, intricate circular geometric medallions integrated into the horizontal surfaces.

### Part 3: The Aquatic Environment

*   **The Submerged Void:** The entire target is immersed in an open-water marine environment, bathed in deep, monochromatic blue and aquamarine light filtered through the water column.
*   **Marine Life:** The engineered concrete structures act as an artificial reef, providing a habitat for sea life and supporting the active, visible growth of reef-building corals directly on the architecture.
*   **Kinetic Activity:** Human presence is strictly limited to scuba divers; certified divers navigate weightlessly around and through the submerged arches and monuments.

### Part 4: Additional Surrounding Information (Environmental & Logistical Context)

*   **Location & Scale:** Situated 3.25 miles (5.2 km) off the coast of Florida's Key Biscayne, the initial phase occupies a half-acre (0.20 ha) space capable of accommodating 850 remains. An active expansion is underway to cover 16 acres (6.5 hectares) with an eventual capacity for over 125,000 remains.
*   **Engineering Constraints:** Due to the extreme weather conditions of the region, the memorial was strictly engineered to withstand a Category 5 hurricane (the strongest storm in 100 years). This requirement was prompted by the destructive passage of Hurricane Andrew during the project's permitting phase.
*   **Management Zone & Approvals:** The Atlantis Reef Project underwent an extensive permitting process involving the EPA, DERM, NOAA, Florida Fish and Wildlife, and the Army Corps of Engineers. It exists within a special management zone where boaters and divers are welcomed, but extractive activities such as fishing and lobster-taking are strictly prohibited to protect the developing ecosystem.
