# Target 0100

**Target ID:** 404K UA26  
**Date of the target:** August 16, 2026  

**Target:** The Pochaina book market in Kyiv, Ukraine, during and immediately following a destructive missile strike.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a major urban fire and its immediate aftermath. The scene captures the catastrophic thermal and kinetic destruction of a large commercial market complex, characterized by towering nighttime flames and the subsequent daylight reality of charred, twisted metal infrastructure and active emergency response.

---

### Part 1: The Incendiary Event (Night)

*   **The Fire:** During the early morning hours, a massive blaze engulfed the market following a Russian missile attack. 
*   **Atmospheric Impact:** The visual data from the night of the event shows towering, intense orange and yellow flames completely consuming a long row of single-story commercial pavilions. Thick, aggressively billowing plumes of dense black smoke rise high into the illuminated night sky, obscuring the surrounding urban environment.
*   **The Energy:** The thermal output is extreme, radiating intensely against the dark sky and reflecting off the wet pavement below where initial emergency crews are positioned.

### Part 2: Structural Devastation (Daylight Aftermath)

*   **The Ruins:** The daylight aftermath reveals the total destruction of the site. The commercial pavilions, which previously housed hundreds of retail stalls and books, have been reduced to skeletal, severely warped metal frameworks. 
*   **The Debris Field:** The roof structures have entirely collapsed inward. The ground is thickly coated in a monochromatic layer of black ash, flooded debris, and the incinerated remnants of vendors' property. 
*   **Loss of Integrity:** The physical tension of the original structures has been broken by the extreme heat, leaving only blackened, fragile iron and steel beams standing at irregular angles.

### Part 3: Emergency Response

*   **Human Presence:** Human activity is defined by emergency service personnel actively working within the hazardous site. Three individuals were reported injured in the overarching attack.
*   **The Firefighters:** Responders wearing heavy, dark protective turnout gear and bright safety helmets (yellow and red) are navigating the treacherous, water-logged debris field. 
*   **The Equipment:** They are utilizing thick, bright yellow heavy-duty hoses, dragging them across the ash-covered ground to spray pressurized water and extinguish the remaining smoldering hotspots within the ruined metal skeletons.
