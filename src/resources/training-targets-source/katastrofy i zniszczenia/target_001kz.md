# Target 0087

**Target ID:** 519N JP45  
**Date of the target:** August 9, 1945 (11:02 a.m. local time)  

**Target:** The atomic bombing of Nagasaki, Japan, as viewed from a high-altitude observation aircraft.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of the immediate aftermath of a catastrophic nuclear detonation. The scene is captured from an extreme high-altitude aerial perspective (approximately 30,000 feet), looking out across the atmosphere. The focal point is a colossal, violently expanding column of vaporized debris, smoke, and irradiated dust—a "mushroom cloud"—towering tens of thousands of feet into the sky and completely dominating the natural landscape.

---

### Part 1: The Central Structure (The Mushroom Cloud)

*   **The Column (Stem):** Rising vertically from the obscured ground zero is a massive, thick, and highly textured pillar of dark smoke and debris. The column appears churned and violently chaotic, containing thousands of tons of pulverized earth and incinerated material being drawn upward by extreme thermal convection.
*   **The Cap:** At the top of the dark central column, the structure blooms outward into an immense, bulbous, dome-like cap. This upper section is lighter in color (stark white and light grey) as the superheated water vapor and debris cool and expand horizontally upon reaching the upper limits of the troposphere.
*   **Scale and Mass:** The sheer scale of the anomalous structure is monumental, dwarfing the natural atmospheric weather patterns surrounding it.

### Part 2: The Aerial Perspective and Atmosphere

*   **The Vantage Point:** The perspective is completely detached from the ground, suspended at a high altitude in a fixed-wing observation aircraft. The camera looks slightly downward at the base of the cloud, but horizontally at the massive expanding cap.
*   **Natural Clouds:** Scattered around the lower and middle elevations of the image are natural, bright white cumulus clouds. These natural formations provide a stark visual contrast to the dark, violent, and unnatural verticality of the nuclear column pushing through them.

### Part 3: The Obscured Terrain

*   **Ground Zero:** The actual city and terrain directly beneath the detonation (the Urakami Valley) are entirely obscured from this perspective by the base of the massive smoke column and the natural cloud cover. 
*   **The Horizon:** The distant horizon is faintly visible in the background, showing patches of land and sea, highlighting the extreme height of the blast column which stretches from the surface well into the upper atmosphere.
