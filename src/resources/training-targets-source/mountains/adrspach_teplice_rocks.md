**Target:** Adršpašsko-teplické skály (Rock City), Czech Republic.

**Description:**
The target is the Adršpašsko-teplické skály National Nature Reserve, located in the Czech Republic near the towns of Adršpach and Teplice nad Metují. It is the largest rock city in the Czech Republic and a unique sandstone massif forming part of the Table Mountains. The location is a highly organized, ticketed tourist attraction of significant natural and geological importance.

---

### Tourist infrastructure and trails

Basic sightseeing information indicates that exploration takes place along strictly designated trails; leaving the paths is prohibited except for climbers with a special permit.

* **Main trail (Adršpach):** Marked in green, it is 3.5 km long and takes approximately three hours to walk. It passes the best-known rock formations. The route is highly developed, with stairs, wooden and metal footbridges, ladders, and protective barriers, making it accessible even to inexperienced hikers.
* **Other trails:** The Pískovna route is marked blue and is 1.5 km long as a barrier-free trail. A yellow 3.5 km route connects through Vlčí rokle (Wolf Gorge), while another blue route around the Teplice Rocks is 6 km long.
* **Facilities:** At the foot of the massif, for example near Pískovna and the Adršpach entrance, are main entrances, information centers, and restrooms. Restrooms are not available along the trail itself. Paid public transportation is accessible nearby.

### Geology and main attractions

The massif was shaped by water, ice, and wind, which carved monumental sandstone towers, some nearly 100 meters high, and deep canyons that narrow to only about 50 cm in the tightest section.

Among the tall formations are Milenci (The Lovers), reaching 81.4 m.

The main attractions and key points include the following Czech-named formations and features:

1. Džbán (The Pitcher)
2. Homole cukru (Sugar Cone)
4. Gotická branka (Gothic Gate), a historic gate built into a narrow crevice between vertical rocks
8. Eliščina věž (Elizabeth's Tower)
13. Velký vodopád (Great Waterfall), a 16-meter cascade surrounded by moss-covered sandstone walls
14. Skalní jezírko (Rock Lake), located on an upper level and offering boat rides
16. Milenci (The Lovers)
21. Pískovna (Sandpit Lake), a flooded former sandpit with turquoise water visible near the entrance

### Environment and ecosystem

* **Nature:** The massif forms a unique biotope and is protected as a National Nature Reserve. The rocks are densely covered with vegetation. Mosses and ferns dominate the lower shaded areas, forming intensely green carpets on the stone, while coniferous trees grow higher up, often rooted in narrow crevices and on top of rock towers.
* **Teplické skály, southern part:** This area has a somewhat different character. It is wilder and consists mainly of long faulted walls and canyons rather than the branched labyrinth characteristic of Adršpach.

### Activity

* Hiking along designated, often narrow trails and steep stairs.
* Boat cruises on the upper Rock Lake.
* Relaxing beside Pískovna Lake.
* Advanced sandstone climbing in one of the world's best-known rock-city climbing areas.
