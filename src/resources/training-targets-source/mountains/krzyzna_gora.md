**Target:** Krzyżna Góra in the Falcon Mountains (Poland).

**Description:**
The target is the summit of Krzyżna Góra (Cross Mountain, 654 m above sea level), one of the two main peaks of the Falcon Mountains (Góry Sokole), located in the northwestern part of the Rudawy Janowickie range. This location combines rich natural features, rugged granite rock formations, and historic tourist infrastructure.

---

### Natural environment and landscape

* **Vegetation:** In the lower sections, the trail passes through dense, lush deciduous and mixed forest. Large old trees are visible, some with carved initials and tourist graffiti, together with undergrowth rich in ferns and mosses.
* **Geology:** The mountain is built of Karkonosze granite. Approaching the summit, massive vertical walls and boulders emerge from the forest, giving the site a rugged character. Numerous other rock formations and crags are scattered throughout the surrounding area.
* **Panorama:** The observation platform at the summit offers a broad view of surrounding valleys, meadows, built-up areas, and distant mountain ranges.

### Tourist infrastructure and trail

* **Approach:** The route develops from a dirt forest path into a steep ascent. The final section to the summit requires climbing steep, irregular steps carved directly into the granite rock.
* **Safety features:** A characteristic part of the trail infrastructure is a set of tubular metal barriers and handrails painted green. They accompany visitors along the steep stair sections and secure the observation platform at the summit.
* **Educational amenities:** An information board with a labeled panorama is installed at the viewpoint, helping visitors identify visible peaks and towns.

### Main attractions and activity

* **Rocky summit and cast-iron cross:** The summit is a distinct exposed granite outcrop, visually surrounded by the tops of the trees below. Its central and most recognizable feature is a massive seven-meter cast-iron cross dating to 1830, mounted directly into the rock. It rises over the observation platform and serves as a historic monument founded by Princess Maria Anna Amalie.
* **Castle ruins:** Near the foot of the rocky summit are historic stone ruins of a medieval castle (Sokolec Castle), although they are now almost invisible and heavily overgrown.
* **Human activity:** The site is actively visited by hikers and tourists using the stairs and viewing platforms. Because of the abundance of vertical rock walls nearby, the area is also highly popular with rock climbers.
