**Target:** Eiffel Tower, Paris, France.

**Description:**
The target is a fact-based profile of a monumental tower, the tallest structure in Paris and an iconic symbol of France. The scene shows a massive iron lattice structure rising directly beside the river and extensive parkland, observed in daylight amid heavy pedestrian activity.

### Part 1: Architecture and construction

* **Structure:** The building is a gigantic open-lattice riveted framework.
* **Materials and weight:** It was constructed from wrought iron. The structure consists of 18,038 metal parts joined by approximately 2.5 million rivets. Its total mass including the concrete pillars is approximately 10,100 tonnes, while the metal lattice itself weighs 7,300 tonnes.
* **Dimensions and foundations:** The tower reaches 330 meters in height including the antenna mounted at the top, and its height can vary by 18 cm depending on temperature. The structure rests on four massive bases forming giant arches at ground level and defining a square 125 meters on each side. The tower exerts a pressure of only about 4.5 tonnes per square meter on the ground.

### Part 2: Spatial layout and functional elements

* **Observation levels:** The tower has three main levels or observation terraces at heights of 57.63 m, 115.73 m, and 276.13 m.
* **Access:** The first two levels are wide and visibly divide the tower into sections. They can be reached by stairs or elevators. The third and highest level is accessible only by elevators. A total of 1,665 steps lead toward the top.
* **Top section:** The summit ends in a sharp mast carrying antenna infrastructure. Two rotating searchlights are also located there and function as beacons at night.

### Part 3: Surroundings and environment

* **Location:** The structure stands in the western part of the city, directly beside the River Seine and Pont d'Iéna.
* **Park:** On the southeastern side, the tower adjoins the extensive park known as the Champ de Mars.
* **Ground:** The area around the base and throughout the park is characterized by broad, light-colored sand-and-gravel paths and large lawns.
* **Vegetation and people:** Numerous carefully trimmed trees and hedges form geometric rows along the paths. The site is densely populated by crowds of walking tourists.
