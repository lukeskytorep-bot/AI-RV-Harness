**Target ID:** 118R HI08  
**Date of the target:** 17.08.2026 

**Target:** Mānana Island (Rabbit Island) and Kāohikaipu, as viewed from a low-angle maritime perspective off the coast of Oahu, Hawaii.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of an uninhabited volcanic tuff cone and a smaller adjacent islet situated in the open ocean. The scene is captured from a distant, water-level perspective, highlighting the stark geological contrast between the towering, pale slopes of the primary island and the dark, flattened profile of the secondary islet in the foreground, all set beneath a heavily clouded sky.

---

### Part 1: The Primary Tuff Cone (Mānana Island)

*   **The Structure:** Dominating the midground is Mānana Island, a massive, steep-sided volcanic tuff cone rising abruptly from the ocean to a peak elevation of 361 feet (110 m).
*   **The Profile:** From this specific angle, the island exhibits an asymmetrical, wedge-like shape. It features a long, gradual slope ascending from the left (west) up to a rounded summit, before dropping off much more steeply on the right (east) side, creating the silhouette colloquially compared to a rabbit's head.
*   **Surface Textures:** The surface of the island is composed of pale, tan, and light-grey exposed rock. This arid terrain is heavily marked by vertical erosion lines and interspersed with localized, vibrant green patches of low-lying vegetation clinging to the slopes and crevices.

### Part 2: The Secondary Islet (Kāohikaipu)

*   **Positioning:** Situated in the lower-right foreground, between the camera's vantage point and the main island, is a distinctively smaller volcanic islet named Kāohikaipu.
*   **Geological Contrast:** This secondary structure contrasts sharply with Mānana Island. It is characterized by very dark, almost black volcanic rock.
*   **Topography:** Unlike the towering tuff cone behind it, Kāohikaipu has a very low, flat, horizontal profile that sits just above the waterline, topped with a uniform, thin layer of green vegetation.

### Part 3: The Maritime Environment and Atmosphere

*   **The Ocean:** The islands are completely surrounded by a vast expanse of deep blue, turbulent ocean water. Small whitecaps are visible across the surface, indicating active winds and ocean currents.
*   **Sense of Scale:** In the distant background, positioned on the horizon line just to the right of the main island, a tiny white sailboat is visible, emphasizing the massive scale of the geological formations.
*   **The Atmosphere:** The sky above the islands is heavily overcast. A thick, continuous layer of grey and white cumulus clouds blankets the upper portion of the scene, casting a soft, diffused light over the maritime landscape.
