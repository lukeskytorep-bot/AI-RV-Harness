# AI Center and Viewer Learning

AI Center shows identities, AI Monitor and two distinct versioned memories: Field Guide and Viewer Notes. Each Viewer identity is bound to Profile, credential identity, provider, exact model route and role. Memory never transfers silently to another model/key/role.

## Field Guide and Notes

Field Guide is learned perceptual guidance. Viewer Notes are operational lessons and recurring observations. They do not replace locked identity/vocabulary/protocol instructions. Both have immutable version histories, capacity constraints and frozen session snapshots. Field Guide restoration creates an auditable new version from old content; Notes restoration records activation of an existing version.

Only classic Training updates them after its own Reveal/Viewer Review, before Judge. Ordinary RV, Conversations, Research and AI–AI Telepathic Training consume permitted snapshots read-only. Notes reflection receives the exact session Viewer prompt and exact post-update/preserved Guide checkpoint; a later active version cannot substitute for it.

## Failed updates

Each memory kind has at most two dispatched requests per session/packet across transport retry and all repairs. An invalid second proposal is failed_preserved, not model NO_CHANGE. The previous exact version/absence stays and Training continues. A failure to create the first Notes version leaves no version; it does not create an empty one.

Training and AI Center show expandable separate causes. Completed Training keeps the failure count/details. Capacity never increases with generation headroom and documents are not truncated. Current headroom is C+32,768 → C+49,152. Stop, DB/ownership/hash/identity errors and uncertain dispatch remain interruptions.

Research freezes its chosen Notes/Guide at Lock, including exact content/version/hash. Prompt Research can compare 2–4 selections from the six most recent exact-identity/language Guide versions. Research never creates learning versions.
