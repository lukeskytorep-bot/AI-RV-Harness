# Sessions and Protocols

## Choose the right surface

Conversations is ordinary chat. RV Sessions contains Manual RV and controlled Automatic RV. Manual RV reuses the chat engine with its own Viewer policy; it does not become an automatic phase controller. Training and Research coordinate saved Sessions with their own checkpoint/frozen-state rules.

Supported automatic paths include Full RCP, RV Lite Core/Extended and compatible versioned custom protocols. RV Lite uses four Viewer calls; its bundled Prompt 3 includes Deepening and Prompt 4 covers Functional Sketches. Supported Monitor work remains separate from Viewer evidence.

## Evidence and Reveal

The blind record is sealed before Reveal. Viewer Review, Monitor Review, Judge and later operator discussion cannot rewrite it. Sessions freeze identity, prompt, learning, route and allowed generation policy. Partial stream text is provisional; it cannot advance a phase just because it is visible.

Stop cancels active work and prevents later dispatch. Resume uses shared step/fingerprint/attempt decisions and replays accepted results before the first missing call. Known rejected finished output differs from unknown dispatch; uncertainty needs explicit resolution. End is a permanent execution decision, not successful completion or deletion.

Manual RV prefers 32,768 → 49,152; ordinary automatic blind Viewer prefers 16,384 → 32,768. Model/endpoint/context capacity applies. Older frozen Research or telepathic policies are not silently updated.

## Telepathic exchange

New fixed-lead series freeze participants, a selected lead and per-round geography. Each round locks its own target before blind descriptions. Up to three lead-guided cycles may precede Reveal/reflections. Receivers do not receive the hidden target/geography or others' blind answers before Reveal. The operator has a broader observer view. New decisions use `fixed_lead_guidance_v2`: one separately addressed `individual` instruction per active Viewer, or an explicit finish for that Viewer. The whole round can end with an empty instruction list. Identical questions must be repeated as separate addressed entries. Saved v1 decisions and requests keep their original contract, including accepted broadcasts. Legacy series retain their protocol and saved policy.

Provider reasoning/continuation is kept outside visible blind evidence. Markdown records are isolated so malformed formatting in one response cannot consume the next. See [Providers](Providers-Models-and-AI-Monitor.md) and [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md).
